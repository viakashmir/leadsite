# Holidify + TripCrafters: competitor analysis (1 Nov 2026)

Holidify (consumer site) and TripCrafters (its B2B lead store, "by Holidify")
run the same business as Tavel Package: travellers fill one enquiry form, up
to 3 agents get the lead, agents pay per lead. This file records what they do,
where they are weak, and what we build to beat them. Evidence: live crawl of
holidify.com and tripcrafters.com on 1 Nov 2026 (sitemaps, raw HTML, JSON-LD).

## 2. On-page SEO, AEO and GEO signals (sampled 13 templates)

What they do well:
- Breadth: every place gets 8 to 12 sibling pages (packages, best time, how to reach, things to do, hotels, photos, map, agents, Q&A).
- Strong answer blocks: "What is the best time to visit Manali?", "Is Rohtang Pass open now?", "Must Know Before You Visit", monthly weather table, editorial rating ("4.8/5, What we like").
- Social proof: "23,000+ Ratings & Reviews", agent ratings, package counts ("271 found").
- Commercial internal linking: every guide page embeds 6 to 7 package cards and "Browse Package Collections".
- Origin pages for routes (Mumbai to Manali by flight, road, bus, train) with tables.
- Multi-currency switcher (INR, USD, GBP, AED, EUR, SGD).

Where they are weak (verified in the HTML):
- **Broken FAQ schema on package pages**: the FAQPage JSON-LD on `/places/manali/packages.html` and the state packages page fails to parse (invalid control characters), so their highest-value commercial pages get no FAQ rich results.
- **Wrong schema types**: Rohtang Pass is marked `LocalBusiness`, not `TouristAttraction`. Package detail pages, travel-agent list pages and how-to-reach pages have **no schema at all**.
- **No Article / dateModified on guides**: place, best-time and sightseeing pages carry no `dateModified` or author schema (only collections and visa articles do). AI engines weight freshness and attribution.
- **Agent profiles are noindex** and carry self-published `aggregateRating`.
- **Duplicate content**: `/question/...` URLs return the full Manali guide with the same title; `/places/places/manali/...` duplicates appear in the sitemap.
- **Stale, malformed sitemap**: lastmod April 2025, `</lastmod>>` typos, mixed http/https.
- **Heavy pages**: 236 to 548 KB of HTML per page; up to 197 images on one page.
- **No llms.txt** on either domain. Duplicate H1s on sightseeing and package pages.
- Unverifiable sales claims in titles ("Book Best Deals Upto 30% Off").

## 3. TripCrafters (B2B lead store)

- Pitch: "Buy High Intent Verified Leads", 1M+ travellers a year, 10,000+ agents, 15+ years, agents worldwide (country dropdown of 230+).
- Verification: OTP + WhatsApp Business API check, "AI-based data checks" on 30+ fields, traveller chooses how many quotes (up to 3), "less than 30% of leads are verified".
- Lead data: name, email, phone, home city, date, duration, group size, hotel type, passport availability, special requests.
- Pricing: from ₹100 per lead, **priced by the trip value**; 20% bonus credits on first purchase, 15% on next two; 15% discount on auto-buy and on leads from the agent's own packages.
- Agent tools: auto-buy with filters, leads to inbox, dedicated account manager, free agent profile and packages listed on Holidify, traveller is WhatsApp-introduced to the matched agent's profile.
- SEO: almost none. 12 links on the homepage, no canonical, no meta description, no schema, all five sitemaps return empty files.

## 4. Where Tavel Package already stands

Ahead today: valid site-wide Organization/WebSite graph, Article + author + dateModified on every guide, TouristAttraction for places, CollectionPage + ItemList for packages, FAQ per page, llms.txt, About/Contact/Editorial policy, keyword coverage gate, small HTML.

Behind today: ~1,000 indexable pages vs 65,000; no traveller reviews, no agent profiles, no photos/map pages, no collections, no visa pages, no origin route pages, no OTP/WhatsApp verification, flat lead price (100/200), no auto-buy, no international agent onboarding or multi-currency credits.

## 5. Build plan to beat them

We will not out-publish 65,000 pages quickly. We beat them on lead quality per
rupee for agents, and on accuracy, freshness and machine readability per page,
then close the breadth gap template by template.

### A. Lead engine (sell leads worldwide)
1. OTP + WhatsApp verification on every enquiry; intent score from trip fields (dates, duration, budget vs destination, group).
2. Lead price by trip value and destination tier (replace flat 100/200), shown to the agent before purchase.
3. Agent auto-buy rules (destinations, budget, group size, dates), lead alerts by email and WhatsApp.
4. Public agent profiles (indexable) with verified-booking reviews, destinations served, licence/GST/IATA badges; traveller sees matched agents right after submitting.
5. Agents upload their own packages (TouristTrip + Offer schema), linked from guide pages.
6. International agents: country, currency and payment options (Razorpay international or Stripe), credits in USD/AED/EUR.
7. A B2B landing section (`/for-travel-agents`) with real lead data: monthly enquiries by destination, average group size and trip value.

### B. SEO templates they have that we need (each with valid schema and dates)
1. Origin route pages: `/{dest}-tourism/{city}/how-to-reach/{origin}-to-{city}` for top 10 origins (flight, train, road, bus tables).
2. Collections: weekend getaways from each metro, month-wise places (India and abroad), honeymoon by month, budget trips. These cover our generic gap keywords ("places to visit in May in India", "2 day trip from Mumbai").
3. Visa guides for Indians for every international destination we sell.
4. Photos pages (real, licensed photos only) and map pages; these cover the "images" and "map" keywords.
5. Travel agents per destination (ItemList of TravelAgency), indexable.
6. Things to do per city with ACTIVITY places.

### C. Where we stay 10x better per page
- Valid JSON-LD everywhere, checked in CI (parse every JSON-LD block of every sitemap URL).
- Correct types: TouristAttraction, TouristDestination, TouristTrip, TravelAgency, Article, FAQPage, ItemList, BreadcrumbList.
- Visible dates and editorial byline; editorial policy; sources named for permits, fees and weather.
- Original data AI engines can cite: our own enquiry statistics per destination and month (no personal data), refreshed monthly.
- Fast pages: keep HTML under 150 KB, images lazy and sized.
- Fresh sitemap with real lastmod; llms.txt kept current; no duplicate URLs.
- Keyword coverage gate stays green for every batch.
