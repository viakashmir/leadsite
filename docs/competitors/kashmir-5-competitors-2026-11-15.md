# Kashmir: five-competitor SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO study (2026-11-15)

Competitors: tripmore.in, pandatoursandtravels.com, sotc.in, travelcoffee.in,
kinghillstravels.com. Goal set by the owner: beat all five on every Kashmir page
and every Kashmir keyword.

## How this was done

- Every Kashmir URL in each competitor's sitemap was crawled live (plus the
  Kashmir links on the five pages the owner named): **940 pages**.
  Tripmore 471, Panda 256, Travel Coffee 144, SOTC 59, Kinghills 10.
- Our side: **724 live pages** crawled (all Kashmir pages plus every blog post).
- Each competitor page was paired with our closest page (rules for origin
  cities, durations, themes and countries, then title/slug similarity, then a
  hand-checked list of about 40 topic pairings).
- Scoring is the same strict rule as the TDI studies: **WIN** = our page has
  1,600+ words AND at least 10% more words AND at least as many FAQs.
  **AHEAD** = more words and FAQs but under the WIN bar. **TRAIL** = we have a
  page but it is thinner. **MISSING** = no page of ours targets it.
  Words are counted in `<main>` only.
- Full page-by-page list: `kashmir-5-competitors-scoreboard-2026-11-15.csv`.

Word count is a proxy for "answers more of the searcher's questions", not a
ranking factor on its own. The plan below is about answering more, with real
facts, not padding.

## 2. What each competitor does, and where it is weak

### Travel Coffee (strongest on content)
- 144 Kashmir pages, **every one over 1,600 words** (median 3,783, top 9,523).
- **FAQPage schema on 100%** of pages, median 12 FAQs; 99% of pages have 5+
  question-style headings. This is the most AEO-ready site of the five.
- Prices on every page; 18 titles say "from ₹7,999" style prices; 70% of
  titles carry "2026".
- Very long-tail intent pages: Kashmir honeymoon in November, December,
  January, February; Pahalgam in November, December, January; Sonamarg in
  January and February; Gulmarg without the gondola; Pahalgam without pony
  rides; Gulmarg vs Tangmarg stay; Kashmir with a baby; wheelchair access;
  tourist scams; Valentine's Day; romantic winter things to do.
- 24 place pages at 3,000 to 5,600 words each (Shalimar, Nishat, Hazratbal,
  Shankaracharya, Pari Mahal, Chashme Shahi, Apharwat, Thajiwas, Chandanwari,
  Baisaran, Zero Point...). Ours for the same places are 100 to 600 words.
- 11 origin pages (Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Kolkata...) at 6,700 to 9,500 words
  and 30 to 45 FAQs.
- Weak spots: Shimla-based Himachal company (less local Kashmir authority),
  **no tables on any page**, 31% of images have no alt text, 40% of pages have
  no published/modified date in schema, 49% shingle overlap between origin
  pages (templated).

### Panda Tours (longest pages)
- 256 Kashmir pages, median **5,033 words**; origin pages run 5,000 to 10,600.
- 80% of pages have tables; 84% have 5+ question headings, but **FAQPage
  schema on only 7%**.
- 81 Indian origin pages and several country pages, including about 32 we do
  not have (Faridabad, Ghaziabad, Gwalior, Jabalpur, Rajkot, Jodhpur, Guntur,
  Vijayawada, Tirupati, Cuttack, Rourkela, Silchar, Dibrugarh, Australia,
  Canada, Germany, Mauritius...).
- Weak spots: author is an email address on 245 pages; clickbait titles
  ("Flat 20% OFF", "30% off", "incredible", "Ultimate"); typo slugs
  ("itineary", "iland", "rinagar"); 8+ near-duplicate "best travel agent in
  Srinagar" pages competing with each other.

### Tripmore (biggest Kashmir footprint, Srinagar operator)
- 471 Kashmir URLs, but median only 884 words; **194 pages under 500 words**
  and 77 are web stories. Many activity/destination stubs have about 65 words
  (Sinthan Top, Naranag, Jeep safari, Fishing, Jet ski).
- Strong transactional coverage: 11 cab pages by vehicle (Etios, Fortuner,
  Tavera, Xylo, Dzire, minibus, Urbania), tempo traveller, self-drive, bike
  rental, hotels and houseboats "under ₹4,000 / 5,000 / 10,000".
- FAQ schema on 58%. 43% have tables.
- Weak spots: **314 pages have more than one H1**, authors "Admin" and first
  names only, thin stubs, no answer-first openers.

### SOTC (brand authority, thin content)
- Head page "Kashmir Tour Packages from ₹19,800" has Trip, Product, Offer and
  AggregateRating schema, 28 question headings, but no FAQPage schema.
- Blog posts are short (478 to 1,286 words), FAQ schema on 7%, alt text on 52%
  of images, 5 canonical mismatches.
- robots.txt allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended; has a
  full llms.txt. Their edge is domain strength, not content. We beat them on
  content already (31 WIN); head terms need links and brand signals too.

### Kinghills (small)
- 10 Kashmir pages, 345 to 878 words, prices on every page. We win 8 of 10;
  the 2 "missing" are their own named personal tours (covered by our packages).

## 3. Our side: strengths and real weaknesses

| Page family | Pages | Median words | Median FAQs | Under 1,600 words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kashmir blog posts | 140 | 2,375 | 14 | 33 |
| Origin pages (from-X-to-kashmir) | 48 | 2,057 | 6 | 0 |
| Kashmir guide pages (/kashmir-tourism/...) | 79 | 260 | 0 | 67 |
| Duration pages (/tour-packages/kashmir/N-nights...) | 20 | 297 | 3 | 20 |
| Town package hubs (/tour-packages/gurez etc.) | 11 | 891 | 5 | 7 |
| Package detail pages (/packages/...) | 101 | 692 | 4 | 101 |

Strengths: FAQ schema on 89% of Kashmir pages (vs Panda 7%, SOTC 7%,
Tripmore 58%), self-canonical everywhere, brand byline, every image has alt
text, og:image on every page, lightest HTML of the six (median 157 KB vs
206 to 353 KB).

Weaknesses found in this crawl:

1. **Thin place and sub-guide pages.** 67 of 79 /kashmir-tourism pages are under
   1,600 words; place pages like Shalimar Bagh (170), Shankaracharya (178),
   Hazratbal (177), Thajiwas (147), Apharwat (149), Chandanwari (155) face
   Travel Coffee pages of 3,000 to 4,400 words.
2. **Duration and package pages are thin.** 20 duration pages at 260 to 630
   words vs competitor packages of 2,000 to 8,000; 101 package pages at about
   700 words with 4 FAQs.
3. **Origin pages are the most templated on the market.** Pune vs Kanpur share
   **72%** of 8-word phrases (Travel Coffee 49%, Panda 28 to 41%). Each has
   only 6 FAQs vs Travel Coffee's 38 to 45.
4. **No answer-first opener.** On blog posts the first paragraph is the byline;
   on guides it is a generic line ("Everything you need to plan Gulmarg...").
   Only 19% of our pages open with a short factual answer. AI Overviews and
   chat assistants lift that first answer.
5. **Statement headings, not questions.** Only 1% of our pages have 5+
   question-style headings (Travel Coffee 99%, Panda 84%). Our H2s read "Season:
   The Biggest Single Price Lever"; theirs read "How much does a Kashmir trip
   cost in December?".
6. **Template bug: wrong place in the FAQ heading.** 22 Kashmir-wide posts show
   "Srinagar Trip FAQs" and "Ready to plan your Srinagar trip?" (e.g.
   kashmir-trip-cost-guide, best-time-to-visit-kashmir, kashmir-in-autumn).
   131 posts site-wide have a FAQ heading for a different place than the post.
7. **Hub depth.** /tour-packages/kashmir is 5,324 words / 24 FAQs vs Travel
   Coffee 6,578, SOTC 6,065, Panda 10,553.

## 5. Plan to beat all five (in order)

**Batch 1: fixes across every Kashmir page (code, one deploy)**
1. Fix the FAQ/CTA heading so Kashmir-wide posts say "Kashmir" (and every post
   uses its own primary place).
2. Answer-first block on every Kashmir page: a 40 to 60 word factual answer to
   the page's main question, with a number in it, directly under the H1.
3. Question-led H2s where the section answers a search question (keep H1 and
   titles of pages ranking 1 to 10 unchanged).
4. One comparison table on every guide and package page where it helps
   (distances, months, costs paid locally, vehicle types).

**Batch 2: the 30 place pages Travel Coffee beats us on** (Shalimar, Nishat,
Chashme Shahi, Pari Mahal, Hazratbal, Shankaracharya, Tulip Garden, Gondola,
Apharwat, Khilanmarg, Alpather, Thajiwas, Zero Point, Chandanwari, Baisaran,
Betaab, Aru, Doodhpathri, Yusmarg, town places-to-visit pages). Target 3,500+
real words each with entry times, tickets, distance, season, how long to
spend, what to skip.

**Batch 3: duration and town package hubs** (20 duration pages, 7 thin town
hubs, the Kashmir hub to 7,500+ words and 30+ FAQs).

**Batch 4: origin pages made city-specific** (flights and trains from that
city, typical trip length and holiday windows for that city, connection via
Delhi/Jammu, what that city's travellers ask). Raise to 4,000+ words and
20+ FAQs, cut template overlap below 40%. Add new origins only where the
travel route really differs (own airport or rail line), not 32 copies.

**Batch 5: new intent pages** from section 4 (about 50 pages), each 3,500+ words
with FAQ schema.

**Batch 6: 101 package detail pages** with full day-by-day plans, what is
included, costs paid locally and FAQs.

Rules that hold for every batch: real, checkable facts only; no invented
prices, reviews, ratings or hotel details; no padding (scaled-content risk);
no em dashes or emojis; titles of top-10 ranking pages unchanged; run
check-blog-quality and check-keyword-coverage before each push.

## 6. Decisions the owner needs to make

1. **Prices in titles.** Travel Coffee, SOTC and Kinghills put "from ₹X" in
   titles and on every page; AI answers quote them. Option: show a live
   "from ₹X" taken from our own package prices (no typed fares).
2. **New origin cities.** Panda has about 32 we lack. Recommendation: only those
   with their own airport or direct rail route.
3. **Reviews schema.** SOTC uses AggregateRating. We should add it only when we
   have real, collected reviews.
